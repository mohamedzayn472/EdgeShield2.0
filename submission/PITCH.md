# Two-minute pitch

“A message says your account will be blocked today. Another contains a routine one-time password. Both mention banking, yet the safe action is completely different.

EdgeShield is our local, terminal-first SMS scam assistant built for a CPU-only **Raspberry Pi 5**. A user pastes a suspicious SMS. An independent TF-IDF and logistic-regression detector scores it locally in **0.92 milliseconds p95** on the Pi 5 and extracts observable lexical cues, while application code immediately outputs a deterministic recommended action.

Our core contribution is a strict privacy and safety boundary separating detection, explanation, and action. Raw SMS text, sender numbers, and URLs never reach the language model. Only a typed `Evidence` payload—the risk band, score, and enumerated cue IDs—crosses to **Meta Llama 3.2 1B Instruct (SpinQuant INT4 EO8)** running on the Pi 5 CPU through **ExecuTorch** (`XNNPACK`, `pi5-cortex-a76-native`, two CPU threads). Every generated `WHY:` explanation must pass a strict validator against allowed evidence-supported statements; any rejected, unavailable, or policy-skipped call falls back to an explicitly labeled `Template fallback`.

On our physical **16 GB Raspberry Pi 5** (Debian 13 Trixie, CPU-only), EdgeShield verified `4/4` English and Hinglish smoke cases with `Llama (validated)` output and sustained **91 out of 91** validator-accepted explanations over **605.4 seconds** with zero fallbacks, **6.81 seconds p95** full response time, **3.20 seconds** median TTFT, **13.49 tokens per second** median decode, **1.44 GB (1.34 GiB)** peak runner RSS, and **zero swap**. We also transparently report that 10 minutes of continuous passive-heatsink load reached **83.7 °C** with thermal throttling (`0xe0008`), pointing to active cooling for continuous duty.

We report detection accuracy separately from explanation acceptance. On our fresh 120-message synthetic holdout (12 ambiguous cases excluded), Detector V2 caught **50 of 60 scams** (83.33% recall: 26/30 English, 24/30 Hinglish) with **1 false positive** (1.67% FPR; 1/30 Hinglish) and **98.04% precision**. We preserve our failed V1 baseline and clearly disclose the 10 missed scams, synthetic and spam-proxy data limits, and pending human review. Following PS3 mentoring guidance (slides 03 and 11), Phase 1 delivers one complete on-device SMS workflow; The standalone URL detector is experimental. Network traffic analysis is planned for Phase 2.

EdgeShield demonstrates practical on-device AI security on Arm: private by architecture, truthful about provenance and limits, and verified on physical Raspberry Pi 5 hardware.”
